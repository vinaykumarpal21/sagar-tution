About Sagar Classes

Est. 2011
Founder & Director

Sagar Sir

"Education is not the learning of facts, but the training of the mind to think."

Established in 2011 by Founder & Director Sagar Sir, Sagar Classes has grown into one of Thane's premier educational coaching institutes. Our core philosophy is to provide rigorous academic training combined with individual mentorship, helping every child achieve their highest potential across SSC and HSC board curriculums.

Our Vision

To redefine grassroots coaching in Thane by making top-tier digital learning, conceptual clarity, and disciplined mentorship accessible to every student from Class 1st to 12th.

Our Pedagogy

A structured approach emphasizing interactive smartboard sessions, rigorous weekly testing, continuous performance tracking, and customized doubt-solving modules.

Our Values

Integrity, individual student focus, unwavering academic discipline, and a nurturing environment that builds confidence and lifelong learning habits.

Expert Faculty

Highly qualified, approachable subject specialists with extensive experience in navigating state board patterns and fostering student engagement.
